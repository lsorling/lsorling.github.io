2025 SGP NDP Celebrations

What's these?

Hey there! I’m from Singapore, and guess what? This small country is turning 60 in 2025, and we’re having a BIG celebration! To make this special day more memorable, we booked a downtown hotel that offers a perfect view of the National Day Parade (NDP) without the scorching heat. We also wanted to catch the fireworks from a high vantage point.
We had to stay for two nights at the Swissotel City Hall. If you’re staying at MBS, you’ll see similar sights too.

NDP is a truly special day, one day after the full moon night. We were so lucky to catch a glimpse of the beautiful red moon (for a short while). And in the evening, a light drizzle cooled down the temperature, and we were treated to a stunning rainbow behind MBS. It was an unforgettable experience!
